As part of my final degree project at the Alfacs high school in La Ràpita (Tarragona, Spain), I am investigating the diatom communities of the Encanyissada and Garxal coastal lagoons, in the Ebro Delta. My working hypothesis is that more diverse diatom communities dominate in the naturally-driven environmental regime in Garxal, and less diverse communities characterize the Encanyissada lagoon under hydrologically-altered environmental regime.
